# SOCPLIS: Survival Kit & Kinetic Integration Manifest

## 1. System Philosophy: Kinetic Autonomy
The *SOCPLIS* Survival Kit is designed to transform the primary vehicle into a self-sustaining energy grid[cite: 1, 2]. By converting human exertion into electrical energy, the system ensures survival and mobility even when traditional fuel sources are compromised[cite: 2].

## 2. Alignment with SOCPLIS Policy
The survival kit is a direct implementation of the core *SOCPLIS* mandate:
*   **Merit-Based Energy Production:** Energy generated via the manual crank module is tracked to contribute to the user's standing within the *SOCPLIS* merit-based tier system[cite: 1].
*   **Decentralized Resilience:** By enabling the vehicle to act as an independent power station, the survival kit minimizes reliance on external infrastructure, aligning with the project's goal of autonomous survival[cite: 1, 2].
*   **Modular Ergonomics:** The integration of the Manual Resilience Module (MRM) ensures that energy generation is ergonomic, maximizing operator stamina during long-term grid-down scenarios[cite: 1].

## 3. Components & Functionality
*   **Detachable Kinetic Module:** Utilizes a quick-release dock for use as a manual bicycle or stationary hand-crank generator[cite: 2].
*   **Retractable Tether System:** A 25-foot high-tension cord anchored to the generator allows for off-vehicle power generation while remaining connected to the primary electrical system[cite: 2].
*   **Universal Connectivity Kit:**
    *   **Tri-Device Charger:** Simultaneous charging for three portable communication or reconnaissance devices[cite: 2].
    *   **Main Battery Charger:** Dedicated terminal for trickle-charging the vehicle’s primary battery[cite: 2].
    *   **Front-Battery/Spark Interface:** Specialized connector for emergency manual cold-starts of the vehicle's ignition[cite: 2].

## 4. Operational Postures
*   **Integrated Mode:** Module acts as a dashboard-integrated generator, engaged via an electromagnetic clutch[cite: 2].
*   **Detached Mode:** Module functions as a manual bicycle, providing mobility in environments inaccessible to larger vehicles[cite: 2].

## 5. Stored State: Spatial Efficiency
*   The system utilizes a multi-hinge folding mechanism for high-density storage[cite: 1].
*   The kinetic module collapses into the dashboard assembly when not in use, maintaining cockpit ergonomics and adhering to the project's spatial compression standards[cite: 2].
